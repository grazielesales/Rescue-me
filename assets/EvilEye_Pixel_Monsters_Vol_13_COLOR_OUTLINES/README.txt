Evil Eye | Pixel Monsters Vol. 13 by elesrech
==============================================

Contents
------------------

This asset pack features a fully animated Evil Eye character designed for platformer and action games.

The character includes 6 animations (30 frames):
    -Fly_1 (6 frames)
​    -Fly_2 (6 frames)
​    -Attack (6 frames)
​    -Hurt (3 frames)
​    -Death:
​        -death_hit (3 frames)     
​        -death_fall (3 frames)     
​        -death_land (3 frames)
	
All come in 4 color variations: red, blue, green & purple.
	
Technical details.
------------------

- Character size: approximately 32x32 
- Canvas size: 80x80
- Format: PNG files, provided both in spritesheets and individual frames.
- Outline version only.
- Contains 4 color variations : red, blue, green and purple.
